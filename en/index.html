If you invoice, you have data.

Every Chilean company issues electronic tax documents, and they list your sales and purchases product by product. Add your ERP and your spreadsheets, and most small businesses hold data they never use to decide anything. We put it in order and to work. Where data is missing, we help you start collecting it.

What is lost today The model’s plan

  • Stock and replenishment

    How much of each product to order, and when, so you neither run out nor fill the warehouse with what doesn’t sell.

  • Purchasing

    Which supplier, how much and how far ahead, given prices, lead times and what you will sell.

  • Demand forecasting

    How much of each product you will sell, including the slow movers that sell in bursts.

  • Routes and deliveries

    Which truck carries which order, and in what sequence, within delivery windows and capacity.

  • Shifts

    Who works when, covering each hour’s demand without needless overtime, within the 42-hour week in force in Chile since April 2026.

  • Production

    What to make first and on which machine, to meet deadlines with fewer changeovers.

How we work.

We measure first and charge after. Every project starts from a baseline you sign, with your operation’s numbers today, and ends with the saving measured against it.

  1. 30 minutes. You tell us how you operate today and we see whether there is a problem worth solving. If there isn’t, we tell you.

    Free.

  2. Two weeks inside your operation. We review which data you have and which is missing, sign the baseline (capital in the warehouse, stock-outs, hours, kilometres) and hand you three opportunities with their estimated saving.

    A fixed price, credited to the project.

  3. We put in order what already exists: SII documents, your ERP, bank statements, spreadsheets. What is missing we start recording with automatic flows, so the data collects itself.

    Part of the project.

  4. We build the stock, purchasing, routing or shift model, with a tool your team uses every day.

    A fixed price, known when the diagnostic closes.

  5. Monthly. We keep the model current, check it and measure the saving against the baseline. Without upkeep, a model degrades and the saving is lost.

    Monthly, and optional.

We charge against savings.

A project never costs over a third of its first year’s estimated saving.

Prefer to pay for results? A lower fixed price plus a share of the saving measured over the first year, against the signed baseline.

You get the price when the diagnostic closes, in UF plus VAT (IVA). The UF is Chile’s inflation-indexed unit of account.

We are Matías, Juan Pedro and Tomás. We studied mathematics and engineering at Pontificia Universidad Católica and Universidad de los Andes, and we have spent years on optimization and on models that forecast demand.

In small businesses we keep seeing the same thing: operations run on spreadsheets, orders arrive on WhatsApp, and nobody has time to work out what it costs to do it that way. The tools to work it out have existed for decades; large companies use them.

argmin exists to bring them to businesses like yours, with one rule: everything is measured in pesos, against a baseline you sign.

We don’t sell licences or chatbots. We use open-source software, connect to what you already use (Bsale, Defontana, Nubox, Buk, the SII, your spreadsheets) and leave you a tool your team understands.
