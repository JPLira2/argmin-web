# Surface brief: argmin.cl home (es and en)

Scope: the one-page site, `index.html` and `en/index.html`, rendered from `src/page.mjs`. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: pyme owners and operations managers in Chile (see PRODUCT.md). Action: book the free 30-minute
conversation (WhatsApp, booking link or form). Proof on hand: the live route solve and the stock calculator;
everything else is example data, labelled.

Unresolved: prices (amounts), WhatsApp number, booking link, LinkedIn pages, RUT and razón social, founders'
photos, measured pilot results. All are config placeholders and print as TODO on every build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page owns "your data, rearranged to the minimum": the same 18 marks (orders, products, shifts) move from
the plan you have today (in turquoise, what is lost) to the plan a model finds (in the ground's ink). It refuses the
category page: a logistics hero with a truck photo or dashboard screenshot, then icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: a paper desk seen from above with objects on it, lit from the upper left (revised 2 Nov 2026: the
founders found the flat version read like slides). Navy plates (#1b2a47) for the hero, the tools and contact,
inset with 16 px corners and navy shadows; white sheets at three heights; overlaps where sections meet; the 18
marks as blocks; gradient descent drawn faintly across the navy as the one texture. One meltwater turquoise
(#22d3bb; #3ddcc7 on navy; #00897b on paper) that means waste in every figure and marks the main action, ink
(#111311) for text. Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 900 capitals for
display, Sofia Sans for text. Greyscale documentary photographs of Santiago and a warehouse.

STORY: the owner reads "Tu operación, al mínimo costo.", watches routes untangle and stock rebalance, sees each
service as its own small scene, puts their own numbers in the calculator, watches a real solve, sees the tools
the team would get, learns how argmin charges, and books the conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a navy plate, the descent landing beside the offer. Headline at 8.4vw over the left seven
columns; the supporting sentence, the turquoise "Agenda 30 minutos" button and the calculator link in the
right five columns. A well sunk into the plate below, with the scene controls (Rutas, Stock, Turnos, pause) on its top left and its key on the
top right; the marks and route lines fill the rest.

FORM: a desk of plates and sheets with a working stage; the incumbent world given depth (palette, surfaces
and texture replaced, type, content and composition kept). Pinned by the founders' approved critique, not rolled.
seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
